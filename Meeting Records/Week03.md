In our third meeting for the Software Engineer Discipline and Principle course project, we focused on refining our game concept and developing an initial prototype.

Creating a Paper Prototype & Demo Presentation
This week, we created a paper prototype of our game to visualize the gameplay mechanics and overall user experience. Using this model, we conducted a demonstration for other teams, presenting our core game mechanics and gathering feedback. Additionally, we worked on developing an early game demo to implement basic functionalities and interaction mechanics. This demo serves as a foundation for further refinements.

Writing the Game’s Epic & User Stories
To better define our game’s narrative and user experience, we wrote an epic story that establishes the game’s overarching theme, setting, and objectives. Additionally, we drafted user stories to outline different gameplay scenarios from a player's perspective, ensuring that our development aligns with user needs and expectations.

Next steps include refining the game demo based on feedback, further developing the game’s mechanics, and expanding the narrative to enhance player immersion.
