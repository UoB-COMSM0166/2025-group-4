In our fourth meeting for the Software Engineer Discipline and Principle course project, we focused on organizing team responsibilities and gathering external feedback on our game.

Identifying Stakeholders & Assigning Team Roles
We created a list of stakeholders, including team members, potential players, instructors, and any other relevant parties who influence or are affected by the game’s development. Additionally, each team member was assigned clear roles and responsibilities based on their strengths and interests. This division of labor ensures an efficient workflow and accountability throughout the project.

Game Demonstration & Feedback Collection
We conducted a game demo for members from different teams and gathered valuable feedback on the gameplay experience. Participants provided improvement suggestions regarding mechanics, visuals, and user interaction. The feedback will guide our next steps in refining the game and enhancing its overall playability.

For the next phase, we plan to implement necessary changes based on the feedback, further polish the game mechanics, and improve the user experience.

Game Playtest Feedback Summary

During our Week 4 game demonstration, we collected valuable feedback from different teams. Below is a summary of the key points gathered:

General Feedback

Positive: The game has simple controls and is easy to get started.
Confusion Point: There is a bug in the space jump that needs to be fixed.
Improvement Suggestion: The exit should only appear after collecting all the coins, rather than being available from the start.
Visual & User Experience Improvements

Improve the visual representation of food, enemies, obstacles, and doors to make them more recognizable and engaging.
Add a beginning page that introduces the game mechanics before players start.
Next Steps:
Based on this feedback, we will work on fixing the space jump bug, adjusting the exit appearance logic, improving the game visuals, and implementing a game introduction screen to enhance player understanding.
