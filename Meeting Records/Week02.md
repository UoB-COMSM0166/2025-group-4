In our second meeting for the Software Engineer Discipline and Principle course project, we discussed the progress made during the past week and outlined our next steps.

1.Development with p5.js
This week, we explored and utilized p5.js to design a drawing game that allows players to create patterns on a canvas using their mouse. The gameplay is highly intuitive—players can click to draw circles and use different keys to dynamically change the brush color. Additionally, we developed another program that generates a bear pattern with colors changing over time, demonstrating dynamic visual effects in p5.js.

2.Project Management with Kanban
To improve collaboration and workflow efficiency, we created a Kanban board to manage the project’s progress. We broke down the work into different tasks and used the board to track their status. This helped us maintain clear visibility of the project’s development and ensured effective coordination among team members.

3.Confirming Game Type & Identifying Innovation Points
After reviewing our initial prototypes and brainstorming ideas, we decided on the specific type of game we will develop. Building on the foundation of our drawing game, we identified unique innovative elements to differentiate it from existing games. These could include new interaction methods, special effects, or additional game mechanics to enhance engagement.

For the next progress,we will implement the identified innovations of our games based on our initial testing and discussions.
